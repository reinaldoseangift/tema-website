TEMA's Mission

To coordinate a network of agencies and individuals within the island of Tobago to direct their efforts to the maximum preservation of life and property, in times of disaster.

Our Services to You

Coordinate Emergency Response

Our team acts swiftly during emergencies and disasters to protect lives, property, and the community.

Training and Preparedness

TEMA offers workshops and practical training programs to equip individuals with the skills needed to respond effectively in emergencies.

Community Support

We provide ongoing assistance through outreach programs, resources, and initiatives that strengthen community resilience.

24/7 Service

Our CERT team is on call around the clock, ready to respond to emergencies and provide support whenever it's needed.
