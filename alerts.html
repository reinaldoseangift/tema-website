Hurricane Season & Preparedness

Hurricane season in Trinidad and Tobago runs from June 1st to November 30th each year. Being prepared before a storm hits is the best way to stay safe and reduce risk.

▸ Before a Storm
  • Create an emergency plan for your household
  • Prepare an emergency supply kit
  • Secure important documents in waterproof containers
  • Clear drains and secure loose outdoor items
During a Storm
  • Stay indoors and away from windows
  • Listen to official updates from TEMA or ODPM
  • Use flashlights instead of candles
  • Avoid flooded areas and coastlines
▸ After a Storm
  • Check for damage safely
  • Avoid downed power lines
  • Boil water if contamination is suspected
  • Follow official all-clear announcements before going out
▸ Emergency Supply Kit Checklist
Water (3–5 days' supply) Non-perishable food Battery-powered radio First-aid kit Medications Flashlights and extra batteries Important documents Power banks and charging cables
