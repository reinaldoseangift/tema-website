Evacuation Routes

Route 1 — Windward Road OPEN
Windward Rd → Roxborough → Speyside Light traffic. Est. 22 min to Bishop's High.
Route 2 — Claude Noel Highway CONGESTED
Claude Noel Hwy → Bacolet Bridge → Scarborough Heavy traffic near the bridge. Allow extra time.
Route 3 — Northside Road CLOSED
Northside Rd → Moriah → Runnemede Closed due to fire activity. Use Route 1 instead.
